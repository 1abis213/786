The philosophy

What makes a dish worth travelling for

786 Halal Restaurant began as a small takeout counter in 2008. The Khan family brought their recipes from Lahore — passed down through three generations — and opened on Rue Jean-Talon Ouest. What started as a neighbourhood spot grew into a gathering place for Montreal’s Pakistani community and beyond.

Our tandoor has been lit every single day since we opened. Our spice blends are made by hand in small batches. We source from local Quebec suppliers whenever possible. We are 100% Zabiha Halal. And we still treat every guest like they are sitting in our family home.

— The Khan family Proprietors, 786 Halal Restaurant
Our journey

From Lahore to Jean-Talon

Every dish at 786 carries a story. Our recipes come from three generations of home cooks who believed that food should bring people together.

When we opened our doors in 2008, we brought those recipes with us. The same hand-ground spices. The same slow-cooking techniques. The same warmth.

Today, our kitchen is helmed by chefs trained in the culinary capitals of Pakistan. But the heart of our cooking remains the same: simple ingredients, treated with respect, cooked with patience.

Family owned
